# Thušo AI

Thušo AI is an AI-powered accessibility platform designed to help people access government services and find service locations in Limpopo, South Africa.

The project focuses on improving digital inclusion and accessibility for persons with disabilities.

## Features

### Government Service Search
Users can browse and find government services including:

- Home Affairs
- SASSA
- Department of Health
- Municipal Services
- Education

### Service Locations
Users can view government service locations across Limpopo.

Each location includes:

- Service name
- Address
- City
- Province
- Location coordinates where available
- Google Maps directions

### Accessibility Information

Locations can contain accessibility information such as:

- Wheelchair accessibility
- Accessible entrances
- Audio guidance
- Sign language support

Accessibility information that has not yet been verified is displayed as:

> Not verified

### AI Assistant

Thušo AI uses Google Gemini to understand natural language requests.

For example:

> "My ID was stolen and I use a wheelchair."

The AI can identify:

- The government service needed
- Relevant accessibility requirements

The system then searches the available service and location data and recommends suitable locations.
